Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Short version: KoreLedger does not collect, transmit, or share your trading data. Everything you enter into KoreLedger stays on your iPhone.

1. Overview

KoreLedger (also referred to in this policy as "the app", "we", "our", or "us") is a private, on-device investment journal for iPhone. This policy explains what data KoreLedger handles, how it handles it, and the choices you have.

The short version is above. The full version is below. If anything is unclear, write to us at becipora79@gmail.com.

2. Data KoreLedger does not collect

KoreLedger is designed so that the following never leave the device:

  • Trade records (buys, sells, dividends, splits).
  • Portfolio holdings, cost basis, and realized / unrealized P/L.
  • Notes you attach to trades.
  • Receipt photos you attach to trades.
  • Watchlists, settings, currency preferences, and theme choices.

KoreLedger does not require an account. There is no sign-in, no email registration, no phone number, and no identity verification. We do not maintain a server-side user database because there is nothing for one to contain.

3. Data KoreLedger stores on your device

KoreLedger stores data locally so the app can function. Specifically:

  • Trade records: stored as a JSON file inside the app's sandboxed documents directory.
  • Attached photos: stored inside the app's encrypted documents directory, accessible only to KoreLedger.
  • App preferences: stored in standard iOS UserDefaults.

When you uninstall KoreLedger, these files are removed by iOS along with the app. We do not retain any copy on our side, because there is no copy on our side.

4. Device permissions

KoreLedger only asks for the device permissions required for features you actively use:

  • Camera: used to capture a receipt photo and attach it to a trade. The image is saved only to KoreLedger's local documents directory.
  • Photo Library (read): used to attach an existing photo to a trade. The selected photo is copied into KoreLedger's local documents directory.
  • Photo Library (write): used when you explicitly choose to export a portfolio snapshot to your library.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. KoreLedger will continue to work for features that do not depend on a revoked permission.

5. Analytics, telemetry, and advertising

KoreLedger contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no attribution SDKs, and no crash-reporting services. The app does not:

  • Track you across other apps or websites.
  • Collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, IDFA, or IDFV for tracking purposes.
  • Send any information to our infrastructure, our contractors, or any third party.

6. Network usage

KoreLedger is designed to function without the network. The app does not initiate network requests as part of its core workflow. Should a network call occur (for example to verify an Apple-hosted resource), it is governed by Apple's policies, not ours, and contains no information about your trading data.

7. Children

KoreLedger is not directed to children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has entered personal data into KoreLedger, you may delete the app, which removes all locally stored data.

8. Data subject rights

Because KoreLedger does not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete at the company level. All of the data KoreLedger holds lives on your device, and you can manage it directly:

  • Access: open the app to see every trade, note, and photo.
  • Export: export your records from inside the app.
  • Delete: delete individual items in-app, or uninstall the app to remove everything KoreLedger stores.

9. International users

KoreLedger is operated from the developer's location of record. Because no personal data is collected, transferred, or stored on our side, cross-border data transfer regulations (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR) do not impose a transfer obligation on us. If you have questions about your rights under your local regime, write to becipora79@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at this same URL. Because KoreLedger does not collect personal data, any change to this policy will relate to clarifications, not to new data practices. If a future version of KoreLedger were to introduce an opt-in feature that involves transmitting data, that change would be presented to you inside the app before it could take effect, and this policy would be updated to describe it clearly.

11. Contact

Privacy questions, concerns, or requests can be sent to becipora79@gmail.com. We read and respond to every email.

Summary

  • KoreLedger does not collect personal data.
  • All trade records and photos stay on your iPhone.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
