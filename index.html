A quiet place to think about the positions you already own.

KoreLedger is a private, on-device investment journal. Log every buy, sell, dividend, and split, attach receipt photos and notes, and explore your portfolio through handcrafted interactive charts. Nothing leaves your phone.

Designed for clarity, not noise.

KoreLedger combines a focused trading log with a complete analytics workbench. Every chart and metric is computed locally from the trades and prices you have entered, so the math is always yours to inspect.

Interactive charts

Custom Core Graphics candlestick, line, and mountain charts respond to touch with smooth crosshairs, multi-timeframe views, and full-screen drill-down.

Portfolio dashboard

Surface total value, day change, and lifetime return at a glance. See sector concentration, top movers, and a smooth performance curve that visualizes how your recorded cost basis has evolved over time.

Journal that stays out of your way

Log every buy, sell, dividend, and split in a single tap. Attach receipt photos and notes. Tap, swipe, and pan between watchlist, records, analytics, and settings.

Private and on-device

No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no telemetry. Trade records are stored as a local JSON file inside the app's sandbox, and attached photos live in an encrypted documents directory that only KoreLedger can read.

What KoreLedger is, and what it is not.

KoreLedger is a journal and analyzer — not a broker, not an advisor, and not a live market feed. All quantitative decisions remain yours alone. A built-in risk disclaimer explains this in plain language inside the app.

Price history shown inside the app is illustrative. Where KoreLedger references any market figure, it is derived from data stored on the device. There is no automatic upload, transmission, or sharing of your trading records with us or with any third party.
