What KoreLedger is — and what it is not

KoreLedger is a journal and analyzer. It is not a broker, not an investment advisor, and not a live market data feed. Price history shown inside the app is illustrative; all quantitative decisions are yours alone. The app contains a built-in risk disclaimer that explains this in plain language.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

All trade records, notes, and attached photos are stored locally on your iPhone, inside the app's sandboxed documents directory. KoreLedger does not upload your data anywhere.

How do I back up my data?

Use a standard iPhone iCloud or iTunes backup. KoreLedger's local documents directory is included in your device backups. If you want a portable copy, export your records from inside the app and store the file wherever you like.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual trades inside the app. To remove everything KoreLedger has stored, uninstall the app from your iPhone — iOS will remove the sandbox along with it.

The prices shown do not match my brokerage.

That is expected. KoreLedger does not connect to any brokerage, exchange, or live market feed. The price history is stored locally and is intended as an illustrative reference for the trades you log, not as a real-time quote.

Can I import trades from my broker?

No. KoreLedger is intentionally a journal you maintain by hand. Manual entry keeps the math transparent and keeps your data on-device.

The app is asking for camera or photo access — is that normal?

Yes. KoreLedger only uses those permissions when you choose to attach a receipt photo to a trade, or when you choose to export a portfolio snapshot to your photo library. You can revoke the permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Response expectations

We are a small team. We aim to reply within two business days. Bug fixes ship as soon as they pass review. Feature requests are reviewed against the app's core principles: privacy, on-device operation, and a calm, focused workflow.

Status and availability

KoreLedger is published on the App Store under the name KoreLedger. The app is delivered as-is. Because it is on-device only, it does not have an online service that can go offline — once installed, the app works without an internet connection.

Refunds and App Store issues

Refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com. We do not have access to your purchase history and cannot process refunds directly, but we are happy to help clarify anything about how the app works.
